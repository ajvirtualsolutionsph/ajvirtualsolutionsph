About Me

I am AJ Javier — an AI Automation Specialist based in the Philippines, building custom AI workflows for small businesses worldwide across international time zones.

My background is in finance, compliance, and operations. That means I know exactly which processes drain teams the most — inbox overload, manual lead follow-up, repetitive bookkeeping, content scheduling. I don't do those tasks manually. I build AI systems that do them for you, continuously, at scale, without turnover.

I specialize in n8n, Make (Integromat), and Claude AI — designing multi-step automation pipelines that connect your tools, act on data, and execute tasks that used to need a person. Every build is custom, delivered in 2–4 weeks, and yours to keep forever.

My goal is simple: replace your most expensive, time-consuming manual processes with AI that never sleeps, never calls in sick, and scales with your business.
