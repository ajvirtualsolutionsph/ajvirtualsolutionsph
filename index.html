Deep Dive

📋 Kanban Lead Board

  • Every lead moves through 6 clear stages — New, Contacted, Proposal Sent, Callback, Won, Lost
  • See your entire pipeline at a glance — no digging through spreadsheets or group chats
  • Filter by stage, rep, or date — full visibility in one screen
  • Mark leads Cancelled without losing history — the full record stays intact
  • Lost reason tagging — know exactly why deals fall apart so you stop repeating the same mistakes

💬 Lead Detail & History

  • Every lead has a full interaction log — calls, notes, and follow-ups in chronological order
  • Callback scheduling — set a date and time; it auto-appears on the shared calendar
  • Built-in call scripts — your best pitch locked in for every rep, editable any time
  • Full contact details — name, phone, email, address, all in one card
  • Anyone on the team can pick up a lead mid-conversation — zero lost context

🧾 Invoices & Proposals

  • Create branded invoices directly inside the CRM — no switching to another app
  • Track payment status per invoice — Pending, Paid, Overdue — at a glance
  • Each invoice is linked to the lead it belongs to — full financial history per client
  • Print-ready invoice layout with your business name, logo, and itemized line items
  • Custom line items, quantities, and taxes — flexible enough for any job type

📈 Revenue Tracking

  • See total collected vs. outstanding — no more guessing what's been paid
  • Pipeline value — know how much money is sitting in open deals right now
  • Revenue trends — monthly and weekly breakdowns to spot slow periods early
  • Filter by date range — pull numbers for any period without touching a spreadsheet
  • All financial data lives inside your CRM — one source of truth for your whole business

📅 Shared Team Calendar

  • One calendar your whole team sees — site visits, installs, callbacks, all in one place
  • Month, week, and day views — zoom in for daily schedules or out for the big picture
  • Callbacks set on a lead card automatically appear on the calendar — no manual re-entry
  • Click any event to see full details — who booked it, which lead it's tied to, what the plan is
  • No double-bookings, no missed appointments — everyone's schedule is always in sync

✅ Task Management

  • Create tasks with four statuses — To Do, In Progress, Done, Cancelled
  • Assign tasks to specific team members — everyone knows exactly what's on their plate
  • Bulk status updates — select multiple tasks and move them all in one click
  • Due dates and overdue flags — spot blocked work before it becomes a missed job
  • Tasks tied to leads so every follow-up has clear ownership and a deadline

📊 Sales Performance

  • Live dashboard — total leads, active deals, won jobs, and revenue the moment you log in
  • Lead-by-stage chart — see exactly where deals are bottlenecking in your pipeline
  • Close rate tracking — know your team's actual conversion percentage, not a guess
  • "Why We Lose" breakdown — track lost reasons so you can fix the real problem
  • Weekly and monthly trend lines — spot patterns before they become a problem

👥 Team Activity Overview

  • Task completion rates per team member — see who's delivering and who needs support
  • Overdue follow-up alerts — the dashboard flags leads with no activity so nothing slips
  • Callbacks due today highlighted front-and-center — reps know exactly where to start
  • Recent interactions feed — see calls and notes logged today at a glance
  • Everything updated in real time — no refreshing, no stale data, no lag
