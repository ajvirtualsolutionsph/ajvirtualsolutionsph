# AJ Virtual Solutions CRM — Built for Every Business

## What Is It?

AJ Virtual Solutions CRM is a fully custom-built, all-in-one business management platform designed to replace expensive SaaS tools like GoHighLevel, HubSpot, and Zoho — at a fraction of the cost. It is currently in active production, with over 31 feature stages shipped and running live on the web.

This is not a template. It is a complete, deployable CRM system that covers everything a modern business needs to manage clients, revenue, and operations — from a single dashboard.

---

## What It Can Do Right Now

### Lead & Client Management
- Full lead pipeline with Kanban board view (drag-and-drop by stage)
- AI-powered lead scoring with trend history
- Lead assignment, follow-up scheduling, and lost reason tracking
- Automated overdue follow-up alerts
- "Why We Lose Leads" analytics chart

### Communication & Outreach
- Built-in email inbox with Gmail integration (send, receive, reply, signatures)
- AI cold email drafting — generates personalized outreach and sends directly from the CRM
- Email open tracking
- Facebook & Instagram Messenger inbox integration
- Email marketing campaigns with contact list management
- Automated workflow triggers (send emails, assign tasks, update statuses automatically)

### Sales & Finance
- Estimates and quotes — generate, send, and convert to invoices
- Professional invoice generation with sequential numbering and branded templates
- Online invoice payments via Stripe
- Bills and accounts payable tracking
- Time tracking linked to billable hours
- Income and expense bookkeeping with a full financial dashboard

### Scheduling & Tasks
- Calendar with event reminders, conflict detection, and Google Calendar two-way sync
- Public appointment booking page (clients can book directly without logging in)
- Task manager with Kanban, subtask checklists, assignment, comments, and completion tracking

### Client-Facing Tools
- Client portal — clients log in to view invoices, approve estimates, and make payments
- Funnel and landing page builder
- Surveys and forms builder with public shareable links
- Document e-signing

### Reporting & Intelligence
- Reporting dashboard with charts across leads, revenue, tasks, and campaigns
- Reputation management dashboard
- Outbound webhooks and Zapier integration for connecting third-party tools

### Platform & Security
- Multi-role access control (CEO, Admin, Staff) — each role sees only what they need
- CEO multi-factor authentication
- Full audit logging and secrets management
- Rate limiting, CORS protection, and HSTS headers
- Sentry error monitoring
- White-label and multi-tenant ready — deployable as a separate instance per client

## Technology

Built on a modern, reliable stack:

- **Frontend:** React 18 + Vite + TailwindCSS
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express
- **Database & Auth:** Supabase (PostgreSQL + row-level security + JWT authentication)
- **Payments:** Stripe
- **Deployments:** Vercel (frontend) + Render (backend)
- **AI:** Claude AI for lead scoring, cold email drafting, and workflow intelligence
